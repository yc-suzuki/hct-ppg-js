Task instructions

You will now perform a task in which you count your own heartbeats.

When the start signal is given, keep looking at the dot on the screen and silently count your heartbeats. Start counting from "1". Do not speak aloud and do not use your fingers or any other means of counting; count silently, in your head only.

When the stop signal is given, stop counting and enter the number of heartbeats you counted up to that point.

⚠️ Do not take your pulse (for example, at the wrist or neck), and do not look at a clock or any other time display.
Please rely only on the heartbeat sensations that you actually feel.

The start and the end will be indicated by a sound and a change on the screen. Please turn up the volume.

You will first complete one practice trial, followed by three main trials.

Sensor setup

Hold the smartphone in one hand and cover the rear camera and the light together with the pad of your finger. A light touch is sufficient; do not press too hard.
During the task, keep your finger on the camera and enter your responses with your other hand.
